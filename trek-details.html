Sandakphu Trek

West Bengal, India

6 Days 3636m Altitude Moderate Max 16 People Darjeeling Basecamp
4.8 ★★★★★ (245 reviews)

Trek Overview

Sandakphu is the highest peak in West Bengal at 3,636 metres. The trek offers unparalleled views of four of the world's five highest peaks — Everest, Kangchenjunga, Lhotse, and Makalu. The trail passes through the Singalila National Park, home to red pandas and Himalayan wildlife.

The trek starts from Maneybhanjang and passes through charming Gorkha villages including Tumling, Kalipokhri, and Bikhebhanjang. The trail offers a unique opportunity to walk along the India-Nepal border.

Trek Itinerary

Arrive at Maneybhanjang, check in to guesthouses, and attend an evening briefing with your trek guide.
Meals: Dinner included.  Overnight: Maneybhanjang.

11 km, approximately 5–6 hours trekking through dense rhododendron forests. First views of Kangchenjunga as you ascend the ridge.
Overnight: Tumling.

8 km trek along the ridge with sweeping Himalayan views. Visit the sacred Kalipokhri Lake nestled amid dense forest.
Overnight: Kalipokhri.

9 km, final ascent to the highest point in West Bengal. Breathtaking 360° Himalayan panorama including Everest, Kangchenjunga, Lhotse, and Makalu.
Overnight: Sandakphu.

Soak in golden sunrise views over the Sleeping Buddha silhouette of Kangchenjunga. Explore the summit; optional descent to Phalut available.
Overnight: Sandakphu.

Descend back through the forests to Maneybhanjang. Farewell dinner with your team. Collect your trek completion certificate and depart.
